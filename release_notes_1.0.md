## System Vitals 1.0

The first release: a free health monitor and nightly backup for Windows 10 and 11.

- Watches the CPU, graphics, drives, memory, cooling & power and app crashes, and warns in plain words before small problems turn into crashes or lost files.
- Backs up your folders every night to a second drive or a USB drive, and keeps older versions for 30 days.
- Installs for you only (no admin needed), updates in place, and uninstalls cleanly. Your backups always stay.
